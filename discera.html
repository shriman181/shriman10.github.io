The Platform

Six intelligence modules.
One decisive advantage.

Script Intelligence Engine
Ingests raw screenplays (PDF/FDX) and outputs pacing scores, archetype mapping, dialogue sentiment, and comp-title matches against 40K+ produced films — at scene-level granularity.
Greenlight Forecast
Predicts opening-weekend revenue range, streaming retention quartile, and awards probability before casting begins. Actionable confidence intervals, not vague guidance.
Cast Chemistry Modeler
Simulates audience response to casting combinations using social graph data, historical pairing performance, and demographic affinity clusters — before talent is approached.
Territory Revenue Splitter
Projects revenue by geography — streaming vs. theatrical, SVOD vs. licensing — segmented by genre, language dub viability, and cultural resonance index per market.
Risk & Sensitivity Radar
Flags reputational risk vectors: cultural sensitivity by territory, regulatory hurdles, talent controversy exposure, and IP clearance red zones — before they become crises.
Release Window Optimizer
Recommends optimal release windows by analysing competitive slate overlap, audience seasonality patterns, and genre saturation curves in real time.

The Opportunity

A market ready for
intelligent disruption

The global entertainment industry generates over $2.5 trillion in revenue annually, yet the infrastructure for capital allocation decisions has not meaningfully evolved in decades. Development executives still rely on coverage notes, personal relationships, and a handful of comparable titles.

Discera targets the segment of this market where intelligence has the highest leverage: the pre-production window. Our platform serves major studios and streamers, independent production companies running 15–30 slate projects per year, literary and talent agencies looking for packaging intelligence, and film finance funds performing due diligence.

The AI-in-media market is expanding at 35% CAGR through 2030, and Discera sits at its most defensible intersection — where proprietary training data, workflow integration, and relationship-based distribution create compounding advantage.

The Founder
AC
Abhik
Choudhary
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Abhik Choudhary founded Discera out of a conviction that the entertainment industry's most consequential decisions deserved the same rigour that financial markets apply to capital allocation. Growing up at the intersection of storytelling and business, he developed a deep appreciation for both the artistry of filmmaking and the structural challenges of making it commercially sustainable.

Before Discera, Abhik spent years studying how data asymmetries play out in creative industries — observing how the absence of intelligence infrastructure at the pre-production stage caused even experienced executives to make avoidable, costly missteps. He saw an opportunity to build the infrastructure that the industry was missing: a system that could honour creative ambition while giving the business side a fighting chance.

He believes the best films get made when the people behind them aren't spending the entire production afraid of the outcome. Discera exists to give studios the clarity to back the stories that matter — without the existential uncertainty that follows them through production.
